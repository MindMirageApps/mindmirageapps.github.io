What WalkRound does

A simple split between office on the web and drivers on mobile.

Office (web)

Register the company, manage drivers and vehicles, and review walkarounds and defects from a browser.

Drivers (mobile)

Sign in on iOS or Android, start shifts at the depot, complete walkaround checks, and log defects with photos.

Walkaround checks

Structured vehicle checks so drivers finish the job and the office keeps a clear record.

Depot attendance

Shift start and end at the depot, with optional arrival detection when enabled — not live tracking or route history.
