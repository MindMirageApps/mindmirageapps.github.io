About

Mind Mirage Apps

We are an independent app studio. We design, build, and publish our own software — not apps for clients, not white-label work, not agency projects.

What we make

Our portfolio spans learning, connection, utility, and fleet tools. Current products include Illusio (magic training), Couple Questions & Games (relationship prompts), Arithon Calculator (everyday utility), and WalkRound (fleet walkarounds and attendance).

How we work

Each product starts with a clear purpose, ships through the App Store or web, and is maintained over time. We keep interfaces focused, copy honest, and availability transparent on every product page.
