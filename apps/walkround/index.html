Overview

WalkRound is a fleet product from Mind Mirage Apps for operators who need reliable walkaround records, depot attendance and defect reporting. It is not a payroll system and does not contain advertising.

Built for drivers and office teams

A clear split between mobile sign-in for drivers and web-based management for your organisation.

Walkaround checks

Structured vehicle checks with clear records your office can review and act on.

Depot attendance

Shift start and end points at the depot, with optional automatic arrival detection when enabled.

Defect reporting

Log defects with notes and photos so maintenance issues are captured at the source.

Office web portal

Company registration and driver account management happen on the web. Drivers sign in on mobile only.

How access works

Your organisation registers on the web. Office staff create driver logins. Drivers download the app and sign in with the credentials provided by their employer.

  • Company sign-up on the web only.
  • Driver app is sign-in only.
  • Office-managed usernames and passwords.
  • No live tracking or route history.
