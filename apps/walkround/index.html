What WalkRound does

A simple split between office on the web and drivers on mobile.

Office (web / desktop)

Register the company on the web, manage drivers and vehicles, and review walkarounds and defects from a browser or desktop app.

Drivers (mobile)

Sign in on iOS or Android with a username created by the office, start shifts at the depot, complete walkaround checks, and log defects.

Walkaround checks

Structured vehicle checks so drivers finish the job and the office keeps a clear record.

Depot attendance

Shift start and end at the depot, with optional arrival detection when enabled — not live tracking or route history.

Privacy and support

Policies and help are ready for App Store review and day-to-day use. Drivers get access from their office — not from this page.
