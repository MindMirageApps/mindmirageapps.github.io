WalkRound Support

Need help with WalkRound? Contact us by email and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Email support support@mindmirageapps.com

Drivers

If you cannot sign in, or need your password reset, please ask your office first. Your employer manages driver accounts and can reset your password from the WalkRound office portal.

Drivers cannot sign up or delete their own account in the mobile app. Account changes must be made by your organisation.

Office and fleet managers

For questions about your organisation’s account, driver logins, depot settings or walkaround records, email us at support@mindmirageapps.com.

Response times

We aim to reply to support emails within 2 working days. Messages received on weekends or UK bank holidays are handled on the next working day.

Privacy

For information about how we handle personal data, see the WalkRound Privacy Policy.

About WalkRound

WalkRound is a fleet walkaround and attendance app for drivers (iPhone and Android) and office staff (web). It is published by Mind Mirage Apps. WalkRound is not a payroll system and does not contain advertising.
